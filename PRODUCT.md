# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Analysts, data engineers, students, and portfolio reviewers who need to understand unfamiliar CSV datasets without first writing a custom schema or dashboard.

## Product Purpose

RetailFlow turns arbitrary CSV files into traceable PostgreSQL datasets and explainable statistical profiles. Success means any well-formed CSV can be uploaded, inspected for quality, and explored through analyses selected from its inferred numeric, categorical, date, boolean, and text columns.

## Brand Personality

Professional, precise, and calm. The interface should communicate operational confidence without looking sterile or over-designed.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy generic SaaS styling, ornamental gradients, glassmorphism, oversized marketing metrics, novelty controls, and dashboards whose decoration competes with the data.

## Design Principles

- Make pipeline state obvious at a glance.
- Keep data quality evidence close to analytics outcomes.
- Prefer familiar controls and compact, scan-friendly layouts.
- Explain empty, loading, success, and failure states in plain language.
- Let meaningful data carry the visual interest.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA, maintain keyboard-visible focus, never rely on color alone for status, provide chart summaries and table alternatives, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
