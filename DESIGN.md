# RetailFlow Design System

## Direction

A bright operations room in daylight: crisp white work surfaces, restrained blue controls, dark legible data, and small status colors used only when they convey meaning.

## Color

Use OKLCH tokens from `frontend/app/globals.css`. White is the canvas; blue is reserved for navigation, focus, and primary actions. Chart colors are distinct and color-blind-conscious. Semantic states always include text or icons.

## Typography

Inter is the single interface family. Use a compact fixed scale, tabular figures for metrics, balanced headings, and comfortable line lengths for explanations.

## Layout

A persistent top navigation anchors every page. Content uses a 1200px maximum width, strong section rhythm, responsive KPI rows, and horizontally scrollable tables on narrow screens.

## Components

Buttons and inputs use 10px radii, visible focus rings, and consistent disabled/loading states. Panels use either a fine border or a restrained shadow, never both decoratively. Charts live in plain bordered regions with explicit titles and summaries.

## Motion

Transitions are limited to 150-200ms state feedback. Reduced-motion preferences disable nonessential movement.
